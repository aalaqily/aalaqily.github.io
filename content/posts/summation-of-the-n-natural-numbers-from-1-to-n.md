Summation of the $n$ natural numbers from $1$ to $n$ can be calculated using this formula:

$$ \sum_{i=1}^{n}i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} $$ 

and this formula is the same formula used for calculating the $n$th [triangular number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_number):

$$ T_n = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} $$

**For example:**

- $ T_1 = \frac{1(1+1)}{2} = 1 $
- $ T_2 = \frac{2(2+1)}{2} = 1 + 2 = 3 $
- $ T_3 = \frac{3(3+1)}{2} = 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 $
